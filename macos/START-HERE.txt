Spotify Control 1.0 for macOS 12+ (Apple Silicon and Intel)
Created by Bubby3999. See LICENSE for attribution requirements.

1. Extract this ZIP completely.
2. Open "Install Spotify Control.command". It saves any previous helper app,
   LaunchAgent and settings in ~/Library/Application Support/Spotify Control/Backups/.
3. Open Spotify normally. Allow Spotify Control to control Spotify if prompted.
4. Use the music-note menu-bar icon to open the dashboard.

Open + Hide is the default. Previously saved explicit mode choices are kept.
Other modes are Open + Stay and Open + Stay + Lyrics. Lyrics requests are
experimental; Spotify may ignore the internal route, and some songs lack lyrics.

The installer never replaces, downgrades or pins Spotify itself. Restarting
Spotify can lose your temporary queue. Keep important tracks in a playlist.

This build is locally signed, not notarized with an Apple Developer ID.
No Python, npm, Xcode or other developer tools are needed to use this download.

Stop the helper:
  launchctl bootout "gui/$(id -u)/com.user.spotifyadmute"
Prevent login startup:
  launchctl disable "gui/$(id -u)/com.user.spotifyadmute"

To roll back, stop it, restore the backed-up app and plist to their original
locations, then bootstrap that plist. Your current Spotify installation is
unchanged by the helper installer.
